# Venue: CS/robotics journal — writing

Use for archival computing, ML, CV, multimodal, embodied-AI, and robotics
journal submissions.

## Runtime venue contract

- Identify the exact journal, article type, submission year, and submission
  stage before applying format constraints.
- Consult the current official author instructions when exact requirements
  matter. Treat length, manuscript organization, anonymity, supplementary
  files, data/code statements, graphical abstracts, and double-submission
  rules as runtime inputs rather than durable facts in this skill.
- If current official instructions are unavailable, request them or leave an
  explicit unresolved item; do not guess from another journal or an old paper.

## Writing defaults

- Use the additional space to strengthen evidence and reproducibility, not to
  repeat the same contribution across Introduction, Results, and Discussion.
- Explain the relationship to any prior conference version and isolate the
  journal manuscript's additional claims and evidence when applicable.
- Keep method assumptions, evaluation scope, compute/hardware, failure cases,
  and deployment limits explicit and traceable to the claims they bound.
- This skill handles initial submission only; route post-decision responses and
  marked revisions to `robotics-response`.

Load `../../../references/robotics-ml-publication.md` for domain-specific claim
and evidence checks.
