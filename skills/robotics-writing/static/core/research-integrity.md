# Research-integrity baseline

Apply these boundaries to every venue. They are a general writing contract, not a substitute for
the current venue, institution, funder, or research-ethics policy.

- Do not invent references, data, results, statistics, methods, experiments, approvals, author
  contributions, or artifact availability.
- Attribute prior ideas, methods, data, wording, figures, and distinctive interpretations to the
  source actually read and verified. Do not minimize prior work to inflate novelty.
- Treat generated prose as a draft under author control. Human authors remain responsible for
  checking every claim, citation, number, conclusion, and disclosure.
- Keep confidential manuscripts, reviews, personal data, credentials, and restricted artifacts
  inside the user-authorized workflow. Do not send them to an external service without explicit
  authorization and an appropriate confidentiality basis.
- Determine AI-use disclosure, anonymity, artifact, ethics, and authorship requirements from the
  current official venue and track policy. Do not import Nature Portfolio policy into a CS or
  generic route, and do not assume that a previous year's policy is still current.
- If compliance status cannot be established from supplied or verified rules, flag it as an open
  submission item instead of declaring the manuscript compliant.

For a Nature Portfolio target, load `../../../nature-shared/core/ethics.md` for the additional
publisher-specific gate.
